About Sanguine

Built so no request for blood goes unanswered.

Sanguine began as a front-end engineering project with one goal: shorten the distance between someone who can give blood and someone who urgently needs it.

Our mission

Blood cannot be manufactured — it can only come from another person, at the right time, in the right place. Sanguine is a front-end demo of how a portal could help with that match: donor search, blood requests, blood banks and camps in one place. It is a student project with sample data and no backend.

Every feature, from availability toggles to emergency request feeds, exists to remove friction between "someone needs blood" and "someone gives it."

What we stand for

  • Speed — every second matters in an emergency request.
  • Transparency — clear eligibility and compatibility information.
  • Accessibility — a portal that works on any device, for anyone.

This is a Front-End Engineering-II project

Built mostly with semantic HTML, modern CSS (Grid, Flexbox, custom properties) and vanilla JavaScript with Promises. React is used only where it earns its place: routing, Context API, Redux Toolkit and controlled forms for the account and request pages. Fully client-side, no backend.

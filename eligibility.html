Eligibility

Can you donate blood?

General guidelines below — always follow your local blood bank's medical screening on the day.

Quick eligibility check

Age

Donors should typically be between 18 and 65 years old. First-time donors over 60 may need medical clearance.

Weight

A minimum body weight of around 45–50 kg is generally required to safely donate a standard unit.

Health

You should be free of fever, infection, or flu-like symptoms, and generally feeling well on donation day.

Medical conditions

Certain conditions (e.g. uncontrolled hypertension, recent major surgery, certain medications) may mean a temporary or permanent deferral — the screening nurse will confirm.

Donation interval

Whole-blood donors usually wait about 56 days (8 weeks) between donations; platelet donors may donate more frequently.

Lifestyle

Get a good night's sleep, eat a meal beforehand, and stay well hydrated before donating.
